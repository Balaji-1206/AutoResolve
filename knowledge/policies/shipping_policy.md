# AcmeCommerce Shipping and Delivery Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets explicit operational guidelines for shipping timelines, delay remediations, lost shipment determinations, and delivered-but-not-received inquiries.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all physical merchandise fulfilled and shipped by AcmeCommerce logistics partners.

## 3. Shipping Windows
1. **Fulfillment / Processing Window**: Orders are prepared and dispatched from warehouse facilities within 24 to 48 business hours of order confirmation.
2. **Standard Shipping**: 3 to 5 business days transit time following carrier dispatch.
3. **Express Shipping**: 1 to 2 business days transit time following carrier dispatch.
4. **Business Days Defined**: Monday through Friday, excluding official federal holidays.

## 4. Delayed-Shipment Handling
1. **Delay Threshold**: A shipment is officially classified as delayed if transit time exceeds two (2) business days beyond the promised delivery window.
2. **Express Shipping Fee Reimbursement**: If an Express Shipping order is delayed due to logistics failures (excluding declared severe weather force majeure events), the customer is entitled to an automatic refund of the express shipping fee ($15.00 USD).
3. **Goodwill Concessions**:
   - Automated agents may issue a $15.00 to $20.00 promotional store credit for shipments delayed by more than five (5) business days.
   - Any customer compensation or monetary credit exceeding $50.00 USD strictly requires human approval.

## 5. Lost Package Conditions
1. **Lost Package Criteria**: A package is deemed lost in transit if the carrier tracking status shows no checkpoint scan updates for seven (7) consecutive calendar days after the expected delivery date.
2. **Remedy Options**: For verified lost packages, the customer may select either:
   - A priority replacement order at no additional charge.
   - A full refund to the original payment method.
3. **Approval Threshold**: If the total order value exceeds $500.00 USD, human approval is mandatory prior to releasing replacement fulfillment or issuing the refund.

## 6. Delivered-But-Not-Received (DNF) Handling
1. **Mandatory 24-Hour Buffer**: Carriers frequently mark packages as `DELIVERED` up to 24 hours prior to physical drop-off during delivery route completion. Customers reporting a missing delivery within 24 hours of the timestamp must be advised to allow the full 24-hour buffer to elapse and check surrounding areas (porches, building mailrooms, neighbors).
2. **Post-Buffer Resolution Limits**:
   - **Orders up to $250.00 USD**: If the package remains unlocated after 24 hours, automated agents may authorize a one-time reshipment or refund without requiring a carrier police report.
   - **Orders exceeding $250.00 USD**: Automated agents cannot immediately issue refunds or replacements. A formal carrier tracer must be filed, and human approval is required before resolution.

## 7. Prohibited Actions
- Agents must not alter the destination shipping address once an order status has transitioned to `SHIPPED`. Address corrections must be managed directly via the carrier's customer portal.
- Concession compensation exceeding $50.00 USD must never be issued autonomously.

## 8. Escalation Conditions
Escalate immediately to Logistics Operations or a human supervisor when:
- The missing/lost shipment order value exceeds $500.00 USD.
- The carrier claims physical signature proof of delivery, but the customer disputes receipt.
- A single customer account reports three or more delivered-but-not-received incidents within 12 months.

## 9. Concrete Examples
- **Example A (Delayed Express)**: Customer paid $15.00 for Express Shipping; package arrived on business day 4 due to routing mistake. Outcome: Automatic refund of the $15.00 express shipping fee.
- **Example B (Lost in Transit)**: Tracking for Order ORD-3012 ($85.00) has had zero scans for 8 consecutive days. Outcome: Automatic customer resolution: agent triggers replacement or full refund based on customer preference.
- **Example C (Delivered But Not Received under $250)**: Order ORD-3015 ($140.00) marked delivered 30 hours ago; customer confirmed neighbors and parcel locker were checked. Outcome: Automated agent triggers replacement or refund.
- **Example D (Delivered But Not Received over $250)**: Order ORD-3019 ($450.00) marked delivered yesterday. Outcome: Advise carrier tracer opened; route ticket for human approval; do not issue immediate automated refund.
