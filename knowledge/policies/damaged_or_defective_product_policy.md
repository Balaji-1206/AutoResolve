# AcmeCommerce Damaged and Defective Product Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines the procedures, evidence standards, and resolution pathways for merchandise that arrives damaged in transit or exhibits manufacturing defects.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all physical goods shipped by AcmeCommerce or partner logistics facilities reported by customers as damaged, broken, or defective.

## 3. Reporting Window
1. **Initial Notification Period**: Damage or manufacturing defects must be reported within fourteen (14) calendar days of the confirmed delivery date (`delivered_at`).
2. **Post-14-Day Claims**: Claims submitted beyond 14 calendar days cannot be processed as transit damage; customers must be directed to standard 30-day return procedures (if eligible) or manufacturer warranty services.

## 4. Evidence Requirements
1. **Mandatory Documentation**: To qualify for an expedited resolution, the customer must submit clear photographic or video evidence demonstrating:
   a) The specific defect or physical damage on the merchandise.
   b) The exterior shipping container showing carrier handling condition.
   c) The parcel shipping label displaying the tracking barcode and address.
2. **Hazardous Damage Exception**: If the product involves shattered glass, chemical leaks, or swollen lithium batteries, physical return is waived for customer safety once photo verification is logged.

## 5. Replacement versus Refund Guidelines
1. **Customer Choice**: When evidence is accepted, the customer may select either:
   - A complimentary replacement order dispatched with priority shipping (subject to current warehouse stock).
   - A 100% full refund to their original payment method.
2. **Return of Damaged Goods**:
   - **Items <= $50.00 USD**: Return requirement is waived. Customer is granted permission to responsibly recycle or dispose of the damaged item.
   - **Items > $50.00 USD**: A prepaid return shipping label is generated. The customer must drop the package at a carrier location within 14 days.

## 6. Value Limits and Approval Requirements
1. **Automated Resolution Limit**: Automated agents may autonomously authorize replacements or refunds for verified damage claims on orders with total value up to and including $300.00 USD.
2. **Human Approval Threshold**: Any damage claim on an order exceeding $300.00 USD requires human approval before a replacement is shipped or a refund is disbursed.

## 7. Prohibited Actions
- Agents must never issue a full refund or send a replacement without inspecting required photographic evidence or securing supervisor authorization.
- Agents must never require a customer to physically return shattered glass or dangerously damaged battery items.

## 8. Escalation Conditions
A ticket must be escalated for human review when:
- The order total exceeds $300.00 USD.
- The damaged item is out of stock and the customer refuses a refund, demanding a backordered replacement.
- The customer account shows a history of three or more damaged item claims within the prior six months.
- The defect caused personal injury or property damage (immediate safety escalation).

## 9. Concrete Examples
- **Example A (Low-Value Damage Auto-Resolution)**: Customer receives a $35.00 ceramic mug broken in transit 4 days ago. Customer uploads photos of shattered mug and packaging. Outcome: Automated agent waives return, offers choice of instant replacement or refund, and resolves ticket autonomously.
- **Example B (Medium-Value Return Required)**: Customer reports defective $180.00 wireless headphones 6 days after delivery. Uploads clear photo of broken hinge. Outcome: Agent generates prepaid return label; triggers replacement shipment upon carrier initial scan.
- **Example C (High-Value Human Approval)**: Customer reports screen damage on a $650.00 monitor delivered 2 days ago. Outcome: Evidence gathered; case routed for human approval prior to issuing replacement.
