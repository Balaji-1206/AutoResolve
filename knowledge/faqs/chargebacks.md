# Frequently Asked Questions: Disputes and Chargebacks

### Q1: Can customer support issue me a refund if I have already filed a chargeback with my bank?
**A:** No. If a transaction has an active bank chargeback or dispute status (`DISPUTED`), our support agents are strictly prohibited from issuing direct refunds. Doing so causes a double loss for the merchant and violates card network settlement rules.

### Q2: What happens to my ticket if my transaction is disputed?
**A:** All inquiries involving active bank chargebacks are immediately escalated to our specialized Payment Operations and Dispute Resolution team, who manage communications through official banking channels.

### Q3: What should I do if I filed a chargeback by mistake and want a direct store resolution?
**A:** You will need to contact your card-issuing bank or financial institution to officially withdraw the chargeback dispute. Once you provide formal documentation verifying dispute closure, our support team can assist with direct refunds or replacements.

### Q4: I haven't filed a dispute yet, but I'm thinking about it. Can you help me first?
**A:** Absolutely. Contacting support first is almost always much faster than a bank dispute. If your order falls within our 30-day refund window or shipping protection policies, our automated systems and agents can resolve your issue in minutes.

### Q5: Will filing a chargeback affect my AcmeCommerce account?
**A:** When a formal chargeback is filed, the associated order is placed on temporary financial hold while our teams review transaction evidence. Excessive or fraudulent chargebacks may result in permanent account suspension.
