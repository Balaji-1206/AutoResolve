# Frequently Asked Questions: Passwords and Security

### Q1: Can a support agent tell me my current password or reset it to a temporary password?
**A:** No. Support agents and automated systems never have access to customer passwords in plaintext and cannot manually set or verbally share passwords.

### Q2: How does the password reset link work?
**A:** When you request a password reset, our system sends a secure, single-use reset link directly to your registered account email. The link remains valid for fifteen (15) minutes before expiring.

### Q3: What are the password strength requirements?
**A:** Your new password must be at least 12 characters in length and include at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one special symbol. It cannot match any of your previous three passwords.

### Q4: I didn't receive my password reset email. What should I check?
**A:** First, check your spam or junk folder. If it is still missing after 5 minutes, ensure you entered the exact email associated with your AcmeCommerce profile. For security reasons, the system will not dispatch reset links to unregistered or third-party addresses.

### Q5: Is it safe to share my password or OTP in customer support chat?
**A:** Never share your password, OTP codes, or full credit card numbers in chat or email. Our agents will never ask for them, and our systems automatically redact sensitive credentials from ticket archives.
