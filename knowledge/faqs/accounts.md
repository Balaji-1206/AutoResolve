# Frequently Asked Questions: Accounts

### Q1: Why is my account locked?
**A:** Accounts are automatically locked after five (5) consecutive unsuccessful login attempts within 15 minutes to safeguard against brute-force intrusion attempts.

### Q2: How long does the account lockout last?
**A:** Account lockouts naturally expire after thirty (30) minutes of inactivity, after which you can attempt to log in again.

### Q3: Can I unlock my account right away without waiting 30 minutes?
**A:** Yes. You can unlock your account immediately through our automated support assistant by completing Level 1 Verification: entering a 6-digit One-Time Password (OTP) sent to your registered email, alongside verifying a recent order number or billing postal code.

### Q4: Can support change my account email address for me?
**A:** Automated agents cannot modify your registered email address. Changing your primary account email requires Level 2 Verification and human approval by our Trust & Safety team with verified government-issued identification.

### Q5: What should I do if I suspect someone else accessed my account?
**A:** Contact support immediately. Our team will suspend the account to block unauthorized transactions, invalidate all active sessions, and route the case to our Fraud Operations team for security review.
