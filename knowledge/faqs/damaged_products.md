# Frequently Asked Questions: Damaged and Defective Products

### Q1: How long do I have to report an item that arrived damaged?
**A:** Damaged or defective items must be reported within fourteen (14) calendar days of delivery.

### Q2: What evidence is needed to process a damaged item claim?
**A:** To resolve your claim quickly, you must provide clear photographs of:
1. The damaged product or defect.
2. The outer shipping box showing carrier handling.
3. The shipping label on the package displaying the tracking barcode.

### Q3: Can I get a replacement or do I have to take a refund?
**A:** You can choose between a free priority replacement (if the product is currently in stock) or a full refund to your original payment method.

### Q4: Do I have to ship the broken product back?
**A:** For items valued at $50.00 or less, physical return is waived, and you may safely recycle or dispose of the item. For items over $50.00, we provide a prepaid return label. Broken glass or hazardous leaking items do not need to be returned regardless of price.

### Q5: Why does my high-value damaged product claim require human review?
**A:** To ensure quality assurance and prevent transit fraud, damaged or defective claims on orders exceeding $300.00 USD require human approval before a replacement or refund is released.
