# Frequently Asked Questions: Shipping and Delivery

### Q1: What are the standard delivery timeframes?
**A:** Standard Shipping takes 3 to 5 business days after carrier dispatch. Express Shipping delivers within 1 to 2 business days after dispatch. Please allow 24 to 48 hours for warehouse fulfillment before carrier pickup.

### Q2: My shipment is delayed. Can I get a refund for shipping fees?
**A:** If you paid for Express Shipping ($15.00) and your parcel is delayed by more than two (2) business days past the promised delivery window due to courier delay, we will automatically refund your express shipping fee.

### Q3: How do I know if my package is considered lost?
**A:** A parcel is officially classified as lost in transit if there have been no tracking updates or checkpoint scans for seven (7) consecutive calendar days. If this occurs, you are entitled to a free priority replacement or a full refund.

### Q4: My tracking says "Delivered", but I don't have my package. What should I do?
**A:** Carriers often scan packages as delivered up to 24 hours before actual physical drop-off. Please check surrounding areas (porch, garage, parcel locker, or with neighbors) and allow a 24-hour buffer. If it has not arrived after 24 hours, contact support to file a claim.

### Q5: How are missing packages resolved after the 24-hour buffer?
**A:** For orders valued at $250.00 or less, our support team can immediately arrange a replacement shipment or refund. For orders exceeding $250.00, our team will open a carrier investigation, which requires human approval before releasing replacement merchandise or funds.

### Q6: Can I change my delivery address after my order has shipped?
**A:** No. Once a package status has transitioned to `SHIPPED`, our support agents cannot alter the delivery destination. You may be able to request an address hold or redirect directly via the logistics carrier's tracking portal.
