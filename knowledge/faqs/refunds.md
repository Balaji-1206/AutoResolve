# Frequently Asked Questions: Refunds

### Q1: How long do I have to request a refund?
**A:** You can request a refund within thirty (30) calendar days from the date your order was marked as delivered by the carrier.

### Q2: How long does it take for refund funds to appear in my account?
**A:** Once your refund is approved, it normally processes within 5–7 business days. The exact posting time depends on your financial institution or credit card issuer.

### Q3: Can I get my refund sent to a different card or bank account?
**A:** No. Under our security and anti-fraud regulations, refunds can only be issued back to the original payment method used during checkout.

### Q4: Are any items excluded from refunds?
**A:** Yes. Items marked as non-refundable in the product description—including final sale merchandise, digital software downloads, personalized/custom items, and gift cards—are ineligible for automatic refunds.

### Q5: Why was my refund request routed for additional review?
**A:** Any refund request exceeding $500.00 USD, or requests involving policy exceptions or non-refundable goods, requires human approval from our support operations team. Reviews are completed within 24 to 48 business hours.

### Q6: Can I request a second refund if I was already refunded?
**A:** No. An order that has already been fully refunded cannot be refunded again. For partial returns, the total refund cannot exceed the original purchase total.

### Q7: Do I have to return my item before receiving a refund?
**A:** Yes, for standard physical merchandise returns, the refund is released once the return package has been scanned by the logistics carrier, unless the item was verified as damaged upon arrival.
