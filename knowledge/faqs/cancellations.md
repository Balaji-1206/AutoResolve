# Frequently Asked Questions: Order Cancellations

### Q1: When can I cancel my order?
**A:** You can cancel your order at any time while its status is `CREATED`, `PENDING_PAYMENT`, or `PROCESSING`, as long as warehouse fulfillment has not begun (`shipping_status: UNFULFILLED`).

### Q2: Why can't I cancel my order if it hasn't left the warehouse yet?
**A:** Once an order moves into `FULFILLING` or `PACKED`, warehouse packing slips and shipping labels have already been created and sorted into logistics conveyor lanes. At that stage, automated cancellations are locked to prevent logistics errors.

### Q3: Can I cancel an order that has already shipped?
**A:** No. Orders that have shipped cannot be cancelled. When your delivery arrives, you can refuse the package with the courier or initiate a standard return within 30 days under our Refund Policy.

### Q4: If I cancel my order, how quickly do I get my money back?
**A:** For pre-shipment cancellations, any active payment authorizations are voided immediately. Depending on your financial institution, funds or credit releases typically reflect on your statement within 3 to 5 business days.

### Q5: Can custom or personalized orders be cancelled?
**A:** Custom-built or personalized products cannot be cancelled once manufacturing has begun, even if the item has not yet shipped. Any exceptions require human approval from our support leadership.
