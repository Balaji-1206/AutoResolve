# Frequently Asked Questions: Payments

### Q1: If my payment fails, does that mean my order is immediately cancelled?
**A:** No. A failed payment attempt does not immediately cancel your order. The order is placed into a temporary pending payment status for sixty (60) minutes, allowing you to update payment details or try an alternate card before cancellation occurs.

### Q2: Why did my payment fail?
**A:** Common reasons include insufficient funds, an expired expiration date, an incorrect billing postal code, or an automated security decline by your card-issuing bank. You can check the specific status code in your checkout window.

### Q3: I was charged twice for the same order. What should I do?
**A:** If our payment system confirms two successful charges for a single order ID, the duplicate charge will be refunded. Duplicate charges of $500.00 or less are refunded automatically; charges above $500.00 are reviewed and resolved by our human operations team.

### Q4: My bank statement shows a charge, but I never received an order confirmation number. Where is my order?
**A:** If your card was charged but no active order appears in your account, please reach out to support. This indicates a potential synchronization issue between the gateway and our database, which our Payment Operations team will investigate and rectify.

### Q5: Can a support agent take my credit card information over chat or phone to place an order?
**A:** No. To protect your financial security, our support agents and automated systems are strictly forbidden from viewing, requesting, or entering full card numbers, CVVs, or bank credentials. All payments must be entered through our secure, encrypted checkout portal.

### Q6: How are credit card holds and pending authorizations handled?
**A:** When you place an order, your bank places a temporary pre-authorization hold. If an order is cancelled prior to processing, the hold is typically dropped by your bank within 3 to 5 business days.
